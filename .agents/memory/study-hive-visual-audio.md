---
name: Study Hive visual and audio direction
description: User-stated design and ambience constraints for future Study Hive work.
---

Keep Study Hive’s interface dark, warm, neat, and playful, with a high-school notebook feel and hand-drawn science details such as a volcano sketch. The Pomodoro view should open as a cozy café-window/rainforest focus room with a clear X exit. Keep rainfall gentle and unobtrusive.

**Why:** The user wants a fun high-school aesthetic with hand-drawn science art and an escapable, soothing Pomodoro interface; they also found the previous interface too bright and the rain too loud.

**How to apply:** Preserve the visual direction across Study Hive pages. Closing the focus room should return to the app without resetting the active timer. Maintain low default rain intensity and volume in future ambient-audio work.
