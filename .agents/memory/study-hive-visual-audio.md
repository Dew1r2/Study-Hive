Keep Study Hive’s interface dark, warm, neat, and playful, with restrained hand-drawn science details. The Pomodoro view should pair a cozy café window with rainforest greenery. Keep rainfall gentle and unobtrusive.

**Why:** The user found the previous interface too bright and practical, and the rain track too loud and disturbing.

**How to apply:** Preserve this visual direction across Study Hive pages and maintain low default rain intensity and volume in future ambient-audio work.
